# Case Study — Northstar Services Group

> Northstar Services Group is fictional. All data in this case is synthetic and was created solely for this portfolio project.

## Executive question

**How should management restore margin while protecting growth and customer experience?**

Northstar is a multi-site European B2B services company with 12 regional units. Revenue increased from €228.7m to €253.9m (+11.0%), yet EBIT fell from €32.0m to €20.3m and margin compressed from 14.0% to 8.0%. At the same time, customer satisfaction fell from 84 to 76 and average cycle time increased from 4.8 to 6.1 days.

## What DecisionForge tests

The case is designed to require cross-functional reasoning rather than a single KPI optimization. The engine tests four competing explanations: operating-model fragmentation, low automation, workforce/productivity mismatch, and the relationship between service quality and economics.

## Strategic alternatives

1. Centralize shared operations.
2. Pursue automation-led transformation.
3. Redesign workforce and management spans.
4. Combine targeted centralization, selective automation and workforce redesign in sequenced waves.

## Decision principle

The winning option is not the scenario with the largest headline savings. DecisionForge calculates realized savings, recurring cost, investment, payback, execution risk and confidence, then subjects the recommendation to a red-team challenge and human approval gate.
