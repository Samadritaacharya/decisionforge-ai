# Methodology

DecisionForge follows a consulting-style decision chain rather than a chatbot flow.

## 1. Situation–Complication–Question
The system starts by clarifying the management context, the change in performance and the decision that must be made.

## 2. MECE-oriented issue tree
The problem is decomposed across economics, operations, organization and transformation choices. Branches are intentionally distinct enough to make analysis tractable while remaining collectively sufficient for the case.

## 3. Hypothesis-led diagnosis
The engine tests explicit hypotheses against regional and company-level indicators. Correlation is treated as directional evidence, not causation.

## 4. Scenario economics
Each option is modeled under four inspectable assumptions: savings realization, implementation cost, disruption and adoption. Users can change these in the live app and see the ranking update immediately.

## 5. Red team
Before final synthesis, a separate agent challenges double-counting, causal overreach, downside sensitivity and execution feasibility.

## 6. Human-in-the-loop governance
Two approval gates prevent the system from silently reframing the executive question or exporting a recommendation without human accountability.

## 7. Evidence traceability
Findings carry IDs back to synthetic source evidence. The executive brief includes an evidence trace so recommendations are not detached from their analytical basis.
